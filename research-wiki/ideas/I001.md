# One-line summary

Insert a parser-free latent relational bottleneck into CLIP and train it with targeted counterfactual binding interventions.

## Hypothesis

A latent slot-based bottleneck plus targeted intervention supervision will make CLIP embeddings more sensitive to relation and role binding changes without sacrificing broad alignment quality.

## Minimum Viable Experiment

Compare OpenCLIP, bottleneck-only, intervention-only, and full RelBottleneck-CLIP on Winoground, SugarCrepe, one causal-order or role-binding benchmark, and one standard retrieval benchmark.

## Expected Contribution

A parser-free relational bottleneck for CLIP, a concrete soft slot-to-token targeting interface for intervention losses, and a clean ablation between structured interventions and generic hard negatives.

## Risks

- Counterfactual caption quality can create false negatives unless filtered carefully.
- Slot count K is sensitive and can either leak capacity or underfit scene structure.
- Broad retrieval quality may regress if intervention losses dominate training.

## Outcome

Proposal reached READY status in refinement logs and is ready for experiment planning.

## Failure Notes

- Earlier drafts left the targeted loss underspecified because latent slots had no concrete token-to-slot interface.
- An explicit GNN-style edge construction block was dropped in favor of a simpler transformer-style slot self-attention layer.
- Synthetic edits need a frozen-VLM filter to reduce ambiguous false negatives.
